About us

A marketplace built around the art of fragrance.

Scentora connects buyers with perfume sellers in one simple marketplace. Every listing can carry reviews and star ratings, helping shoppers make informed choices.

Our interface follows the rule of thirds, clear contrast, a restrained warm-neutral color scheme, and a deliberate typographic hierarchy so the shopping path remains easy to understand.
